Why

Thesis in three bullets

  1. The price assumes little growth. The reverse DCF says the price implies 6.0% stage-1 FCF growth. UNH's median rolling 5-year FCF growth is 13.7%.
  2. Revenue kept growing. Revenue grew from $287.6B (FY2021) to $447.6B (FY2025). The problem is margin, not demand.
  3. The thesis is a margin recovery, and it is not yet in the numbers. Operating margin fell from 8.7% to 4.2%. The long case needs FCF to stop shrinking.

Catalysts

What to monitor

  • Next 10-K FCF. FY2025 FCF was $16.1B. The price implies 6.0% a year from here. A further decline would break the thesis.
  • Operating margin. It is 4.2% against 8.7% in FY2023. A recovery is the upside driver.
  • Beta re-estimate. If the 5y beta drifts toward the full-sample 0.78, WACC rises to 10.5%.

No dated catalysts are listed: this project uses only the frozen data and does not forecast events.

Risks

Key risks and what would change my mind

  • The bear case loses money. The bear value of $354.47 is -3.4% vs the price.
  • The discount rate rests on a low beta. The 5y beta of 0.41 gives the 8.0% WACC. The full-sample beta of 0.78 gives a 10.5% WACC and a value of $321.29 (-12.5%). On that beta the long case disappears.
  • FCF is falling. It fell from $25.7B (FY2023) to $16.1B (FY2025), while stage-1 growth uses the 13.7% long-run median.
  • The track record is weak. Buy-and-hold UNH's Sharpe of 0.49 trails SPY's 0.87, with a -61.4% max drawdown.

I would drop the long if the next 10-K shows FCF still falling, or if a re-estimated beta pushes WACC to the point where the base value falls below the price.
